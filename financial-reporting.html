Management reporting

Monthly P&L & financial reporting

Making revenue, operating costs and store profitability easier for management to understand.

ContextSweet Bamboo internship
FocusReporting visibility & controls
FilesPowerPoint · PDF · Excel

The business problem

Management could see sales and total spending, but lacked a consistent monthly P&L and a clear breakdown of the costs affecting profitability. Comparing stores and identifying expense drivers required more structured reporting.

My contribution

I developed the transaction-import process, category-mapping rules, exception review, P&L formulas and layout, with Google Apps Script supporting repetitive steps. The reporting process turns app and bank exports into categorized monthly financial views.

Analytical approach

  • Normalize transaction dates, amounts and descriptions.
  • Map transactions to revenue channels and operating expense categories.
  • Review uncategorized entries, exclusions and source-total differences.
  • Aggregate the P&L and explain monthly variances using a result bridge.

What the sample demonstrates

A two-period comparison across three fictional stores, with a management summary, expense detail and a worked operating-result bridge.

How to measure success

  • Days from month-end to an approved report.
  • Share of eligible entries categorized.
  • Unexplained reconciliation differences.
  • Follow-through on assigned actions.

Establish the baseline at a reconciled close. No quantified P&L impact is claimed.

Inside the management report

The presentation connects the financial comparison to cost drivers and management questions.

Fictional financial inputs. The supporting workbook contains editable data and formulas.

Assumptions that matter

Transaction timing determines the reporting month. Food purchases approximate spending rather than ingredient consumption unless opening and closing inventory are reconciled. Shared costs need consistent allocation rules.

Operating result is a management subtotal: revenue less the eight expense categories included in the demonstration. It should be interpreted using that definition.

Management use

Review food quantities and prices, compare labor with demand, and evaluate channel contribution after commissions and advertising. Investigate the underlying transaction or operational driver before choosing an action.
