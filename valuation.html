Company valuation studies

My forecasts and valuation analyses for Starbucks, Colgate-Palmolive and Costco.

Historical model baseFY2025
ForecastsFY2026–FY2027
FilesThree PDF reports

How I approached the valuations

I started with how each business earns revenue, then used those operating assumptions to build the financial forecast. For Starbucks and Costco, store growth and sales per store were important. For Colgate-Palmolive, I forecast revenue by region and business segment.

I used those forecasts to estimate cash flow and compare a DCF valuation with an enterprise-value multiple. I also looked at margins and return on invested capital to understand what the forecast implied about the business.

Key modeling choices

The growth and margin assumptions drive the operating forecast. The discount rate and terminal assumptions affect how that forecast translates into value. Company-reported earnings also need to be distinguished from the analytical earnings build used in parts of the model.

Reading the reports

“E” means an estimate. The reference share prices are comparison inputs in the models, rather than current market prices.

The methodology notes explain the earnings definitions, valuation assumptions and public filing references. The company figures have not been replaced with restaurant-style example data.
