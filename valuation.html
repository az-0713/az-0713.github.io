Valuation research

From operating drivers to estimated value.

Three company studies linking business assumptions, financial forecasts, cash flow and valuation.

CompaniesStarbucks · Colgate-Palmolive · Costco
Model baseFY2025
Forecast horizonFY2026–FY2027

A consistent analytical framework

I analyzed each company’s operating drivers, built forecasts and compared discounted cash-flow value with a forward enterprise-value multiple. The reports also examine cash generation, invested capital and the assumptions behind the conclusions.

The studies are model-based analyses. E denotes an estimate; reference share prices are model comparison inputs rather than live quotations. Methodology appendices clarify reporting bases, definitions and sources.

Methods

  • Operating and regional revenue forecasts.
  • Margin and earnings analysis.
  • Cash flow and reinvestment.
  • Return on invested capital.
  • CAPM and weighted average cost of capital.
  • DCF and EV/EBITD valuation.

EBITD is defined in the reports as operating income plus depreciation and amortization.
